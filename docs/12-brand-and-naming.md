## 3. Brand Voice & Copywriting Guidelines

| Tone Dimension | Do Say | Don't Say |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authority** | "Potential unauthenticated path traversal identified in `src/view.js`." | "CRITICAL HACKER ALERT! Your code is wide open to hackers!" |
| **Clarity** | "Estimated disaster exposure models potential downtime and regulatory disclosure costs." | "This bug will definitely cost your company $10 million." |

## 5. Naming-Risk Checklist & Conflict Mitigation

Because "CyberShield" is a frequently referenced compound term across enterprise security and antivirus products, the following checklist governs brand protection and differentiation:

| Verification Area | Status & Recommendations | Fallback Alternative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trademark Classes** | Class 09 (Software/Security) & Class 42 (SaaS security analysis). High concentration of legacy hardware/antivirus products. Recommended: register as **CyberShield Code Intelligence** or **CyberShield Security Radar** to establish distinct descriptive secondary meaning. | *Fallback Names*: **AegisCode**, **CipherScan**, **ShieldGraph**, **VigilantAST**. |
| **Domain Strategy** | Primary `.com` likely parked or premium. Target developer-friendly TLDs: `cybershield.dev`, `cybershield.io`, or action-oriented: `getcybershield.com`, `cybershield-scan.com`. | Use `.dev` or `.io` to emphasize developer tooling. |
| **GitHub Organization** | Check availability of `github.com/cybershield-sec` or `github.com/cybershield-platform`. | `cybershield-oss` or `cybershield-audit`. |
| **Visual Differentiator** | Avoid generic antivirus green checkmarks or generic brass padlocks. Use modern deep slate (`#090D16`) with electric cyan (`#06B6D4`) and high-density technical telemetry typography (`Fira Code`). | Establishes immediate distinction from legacy IT antivirus suites. |
